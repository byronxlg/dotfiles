---
name: project-idea-validator
description: Pressure-test a product idea with competitor research, market validation, and evidence-backed go/no-go guidance before building.
---

Evaluate the user's idea candidly. Challenge assumptions without defaulting to praise or dismissing strengths supported by evidence.

Establish the problem, target audience, differentiator, and monetization assumptions from the conversation. Ask for missing context only when it materially affects the assessment.

Research direct competitors and substitutes using current sources. Assess demand signals, market saturation, switching costs, distribution, defensibility, technical complexity, and adoption barriers. Cite evidence and distinguish measured facts from estimates or unresolved assumptions; do not invent market metrics.

Report strengths, fatal risks, and a clear go/no-go recommendation with confidence and remaining unknowns. If promising, propose a focused MVP and demand-validation steps. If weak, suggest concrete pivots or narrower audiences. Provide advisory findings before making changes or building anything.
